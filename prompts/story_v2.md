Write ONE story that follows the plan below exactly. Output only the story text: no title, no headings, no notes, no "The End".

Rules

2. Pronouns. Refer to $A and $B by their placeholders. If a pronoun is needed, use they / them / their. Never use he, she, him, her, his, or hers for anyone in the story. In dialogue, I, me, my, you, and we are fine as usual ("I did it," said $A).

Do not copy the examples, which only show what an ending means; use your own objects, places, games, and situations.

The AFTERMATH line stands in for all of that. End with a simple everyday ending (going home, dinner, bedtime).
